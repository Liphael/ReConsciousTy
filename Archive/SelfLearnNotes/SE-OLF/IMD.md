## Research Manuscript

the essence of information mark down problem is actually how to use a sequential transform(linear transform) of structure to rebuild the structure.

A Descriptive Definition: information mark down problem is such a type of encoding problem, that using redundancy to reliably transmit information over a channel(genus); the information encoded is nonlinear structure, but channel is a linear sequence. The cost is paid by length of sequence and reciever's memory burden, with revenue of restorable structure over space and time.

A Stipulative Definition: not given yet.

related labels: category theory, graph theory, information theory, 

remember the bottleneck limiting here is the working memory, not the bandwidth.

actually, IMD problem not only includes the encoding terminal, but also should consider the decoding terminal; i.e. the types of reader, or say the method of decoding.

nonlinear structure here can be divided into two methods to describe:
    - category $K$: from category theory
    - fixed-point operator on the power set: from Dung semantics of argumentation framework.
